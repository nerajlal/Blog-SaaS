BEFORE YOUR FIRST STORY

A few things worth knowing.

What does BlogSaaS actually do?+

It’s a workspace to write and manage blogs, use Claude with approved business context, create social versions, and deliver published content to your own website through an API.

Does AI publish anything automatically?+

No. Review AI suggestions, edit them, and choose when to create a private blog draft or publish it. Social content is prepared for you to review and share. Direct LinkedIn and Reddit API publishing is not available yet.

Do I need a Claude account or API configuration?+

AI features require the platform’s Claude API configuration and enabled workspace access with a request allowance. The allowance counts requests, not a guaranteed dollar budget. You can create and edit blogs manually without AI.

Will it work with my existing website?+

Yes, if your developer connects the content API. Your website handles the listing and article routes, rendering, meta tags, and cache refreshes. BlogSaaS doesn’t replace or automatically build your frontend.

What is needed for Analytics & Search?+

The platform must configure Google OAuth. Connect an account with access to your GA4 or Search Console property. GA4 tracking must already be installed on your website. Search Console tools and sitemap generation also use your configured, verified public domain.

Does a good content-health score guarantee rankings?+

No. Scores come from local editorial rules, and readability is an estimate. They don’t verify facts, originality, or search rankings. Review the article’s accuracy and usefulness before publishing.

Does the sitemap update automatically?+

The sitemap API generates current XML when requested. Your developer can proxy it from your website. Downloaded XML is a snapshot and must be replaced when content changes. Google submission is separate.
