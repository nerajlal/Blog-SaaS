BEFORE YOUR FIRST POST

A few things worth knowing.

What does TryWryter actually do?+

It’s a workspace to write and manage posts, use TryWryter AI with approved business context, create social versions, and deliver published content to your own website through an API.

Does AI publish anything automatically?+

No. Review AI suggestions, edit them, and choose when to create a private post draft or publish it. Social content is prepared for you to review and share. Direct LinkedIn and Reddit API publishing is not available yet.

Do I need to set up AI myself?+

No separate AI account or API key is needed. AI writing is available when your workspace has AI access and a request allowance. The allowance counts requests, not a guaranteed dollar budget. You can create and edit posts manually without AI.

Will it work with my existing website?+

Yes, if your developer connects the content API. Your website handles the listing and article routes, rendering, meta tags, and cache refreshes. TryWryter doesn’t replace or automatically build your frontend.

What is needed for Analytics & Search?+

The platform must configure Google OAuth. Connect an account with access to your GA4 or Search Console property. GA4 tracking must already be installed on your website. Search Console tools and sitemap generation also use your configured, verified public domain.

Does a good content-health score guarantee rankings?+

No. Scores come from local editorial rules, and readability is an estimate. They don’t verify facts, originality, or search rankings. Review the article’s accuracy and usefulness before publishing.

Does the sitemap update automatically?+

The sitemap API generates current XML when requested. Your developer can proxy it from your website. Downloaded XML is a snapshot and must be replaced when content changes. Google submission is separate.
