What gets imported?

Title, slug, category, publication status and dates, article content, author details, featured image and section images.

  • Use the exported .xlsx format, with the headers in the first row of the first worksheet.
  • Up to 100 posts and 5 MB per file. Formula cells are not supported.
  • HTML is preferred over plain text. Images inside the HTML become ordered image sections.
  • Remote images are downloaded after confirmation and optimized to WebP. Complete embedded image data is supported too. Each image must be publicly accessible over HTTPS and at most 2 MB.
  • Existing slugs are skipped. Posts in other projects are never changed.
  • No AI request credits are used.

When choosing your profile, new posts use your current author details. When preserving file authors, exported name, title and avatar are used. Later editing a post refreshes its author from your profile.
